## What the folder says as a whole

1. **Measure fit strictly and distrust praise.** Count only real use, ignore compliments, and look
   for buyers asking the price rather than fans asking for features (cards 02, 03, 08).
2. **A narrow segment's love is not a market,** and a niche chosen too small caps what follows
   (01, 03, 08).
3. **A product only somewhat better than the incumbent does not move people;** but inside an
   established operation, small gains can compound (06, 07).
4. **The knowledge that matters is in operators' heads,** reached by sitting beside them, with
   pilots measured against a baseline agreed on the first day (09, 10, 02).
5. **When growth stalls, change the message and the customer before the product** (03).
6. **Learning cannot be handed off;** speed of building is not speed of understanding (05).
