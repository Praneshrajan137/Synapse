## What the folder says as a whole

1. **Fit is found with strangers, on the floor, by urgency.** Interview people who owe you nothing,
   spend time where the work happens, name the buyer as well as the user, and ask what breaks if
   nothing changes for a year (cards 01, 03, 04, 05, 12).
2. **A model's report that it acted is a claim to check,** and an independent checker belongs in
   the loop before the person sees the result (07, 08, 09).
3. **Autonomy is set by the kind of work and who carries the liability:** routine back-office work
   runs alone, judgment and irreversible changes stay with a person (09, 15).
4. **Deterministic where possible, models narrowed and checked** (08, 09, 16).
5. **Environments with verifiers are what trains and proves agents,** and where outcomes arrive
   slowly, as in the physical world, the simulator's fidelity is the limit (18, 20).
6. **The aggregate hides the slice, and contracted is not delivered** (17, 12, 19).
