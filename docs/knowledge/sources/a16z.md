## What the folder says as a whole

1. **Judge AI by outcomes, not by deployment or sign-ups.** Few companies measure an effect over
   time; adoption without use is common; people respond to money recovered, not efficiency (cards
   02, 03, 04, 17).
2. **Records and reality diverge, and automation on an unchecked record spreads the error.** Four
   cards point the same way: call recordings contradicting the sales system, unverified billing
   logic, an overnight audit of case files, and counting a storeroom from photographs (10, 11, 14,
   16).
3. **Keep inference out of what a query or rule can answer,** and narrow what the model decides
   to reach reliability (06, 13, 18, 24).
4. **Oversight has to be built in, not watched:** a supervisor before every action, allowlists,
   permissions per subtask, mandatory gates for irreversible steps, and a designed default for
   silence (07, 16, 17, 20, 22).
5. **Honest evaluation is private, held out, run by someone without a stake, and counts every
   failed attempt** (15, 19).
6. **Context beats a bigger model,** and the data that matters is local and unpublished (10, 14).

No figure from this folder carries a SYNAPSE decision, so no web check was made.

**Where the folder agrees with SYNAPSE as built:** the typed A2A envelope between agents,
confidence-gated escalation, the event-sourced audit log that replay can re-read, and a single
seam for model calls.

**Where it argues against SYNAPSE:**

- The twin draws each demanded item uniformly from the catalogue (measured,
  `digital_twin/simulation/engine.py:703`; I found no weighting elsewhere). A store's demand is
  concentrated in a few items, so the twin has no fast and slow movers, and the proof cannot show
  where any policy wins or loses by item class (INS-060, R-59). Cards 14 and SRC-SEQG-07 point at
  why that matters.
- The same hands built the system, the harness and the scorer; cards 15 and 19 say the dissent
  must be designed in, as a role or an outside check (INS-029).
- A decision layer on someone else's system of record has little pricing power (card 10), and
  the "yet" test (card 13) leaves the agents, not the twin and the proof, as the threatened part
  of SYNAPSE (T-004).
- The learn phase closes a loop that card 19 warns can oscillate; nothing yet checks that a
  learned change did not make the policy worse (INS-011, T-003).
